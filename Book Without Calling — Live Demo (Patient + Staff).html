Book Without Calling

Free mobile health van, rural Southeast Ohio. Patients book, change or cancel visits by plain text, with no phone call, no app and no data. Staff see everything on one screen. Choose who you want to be:

Try this in “See both, live”

  1. On the phone, text 1: Linda appears on the staff list.
  2. Text refill my pills: her visit reason shows up for the nurse.
  3. On the staff side, use Move… on Linda's row and pick any date on the calendar: her phone gets the update.
  4. On the phone, text can I come Jan 15th: on the staff side, pick Jan 15 in Viewing to find her there.
  5. Switch texting as above the phone to try another patient, like Ray or Mae.
  6. Text my chest hurts: an URGENT task appears for staff instantly.
  7. Press Next patient message to see other patients texting in.
