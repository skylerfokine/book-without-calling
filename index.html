Book Without Calling

Free mobile health van, rural Southeast Ohio. Patients book, change or cancel visits by plain text, with no phone call, no app and no data. Staff see everything on one screen. Choose who you want to be:

Try this in “See both, live”

  1. On the phone, text 1: Linda appears on the staff list.
  2. Text refill my pills: her visit reason shows up for the nurse.
  3. On the staff side, use Move… on Linda's row to change her time or her date: her phone gets the update.
  4. Switch texting as above the phone to try another patient, like Ray or Mae.
  5. Text my chest hurts: an URGENT task appears for staff instantly.
  6. Press Next patient message to see other patients texting in.

Use it on two devices

Open the patient link on a phone and the staff link on a laptop. Anything texted on the phone shows up on the staff screen, and staff changes reach the phone. Pages in the same room share one demo.
